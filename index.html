GOOD TO KNOW

Simple by design.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. The core calculator runs locally with no network calls. The draft landing page is separate from the calculator.

Does it know my area's market pricing?

No. You enter your own selling rates and actual costs. Included starting values are examples, not market recommendations.

Can it export a PDF?

Use the Print / Save customer PDF button and choose your browser's “Save as PDF” print destination. There is no separate PDF-generation service.
